# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including toward people who are new to game design or to agent skills.
- Critique designs, text and code, not people. The skill itself reviews designs bluntly; discussions here should not.
- Keep other people's design material confidential unless they chose to share it publicly.

## Not accepted

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexualized content, personal attacks, and publishing someone's private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to the maintainer through [a private security advisory](https://github.com/kristianrolando/game-design-skill/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@kristianrolando](https://github.com/kristianrolando) on GitHub. The maintainer may edit or remove content, and warn, restrict or ban participants, in proportion to the problem.
